About us

Technology should not leave anyone behind, and it should not end up in a landfill.

The Spartan Repair Initiative started at San José State in 2026 around two problems that turn out to be the same problem. People, especially older adults, get left out of technology or taken advantage of by it. And the devices that could serve them for years get tossed the moment something breaks.

So we do both halves. We teach, in plain language, to whoever needs it. And we repair, with people who know how, so working electronics stay in use and broken ones get recycled properly. Along the way we are learning how policy gets made, because the rules around repair decide how much of this is even possible.

Programs

Four things we do, all hands-on.

For seniors

Tech literacy workshops

Free sessions for beginners. Bring your own phone or laptop, follow along on a big screen, and leave with a printed cheat sheet. We cover getting around your device, spotting scam calls and texts, and whatever questions you walk in with.

30+seniors at our first workshop, in Los Angeles
Format
Slideshow, live demos, one-on-one help
Where
Libraries, senior centers, community centers
With Fixit Clinic at SJSU

Repair, not replace

Repair events where people bring broken items and learn to fix them alongside coaches. SRI co-hosts, teaches tech safety on site, and helps route what cannot be fixed to responsible recycling instead of the trash.

Policy

Right to repair

California passed a Right to Repair Act in 2023. We want students to understand it and to have a voice in what comes next. We are building relationships with campus and city partners, and we count what our events repair and divert so the case for repair is backed by local numbers.

How
Teach-ins, public comment, partnerships
Goal
Less e-waste on campus and across San José
For kids

Youth workshops

Short, hands-on sessions that get kids thinking about where their devices come from, what happens when they break, and how to take care of the planet. In development now with SJSU student volunteers.
